How to fly

Dive for speed, climb to slow down — stall and the nose drops. Skimming the ground is safe; towers and trees are not. Water soaks your paper: swim briefly, then take off and dry out. Press fire to shoot lasers. Hold the brake to stall and fall: a gentle touchdown lets you rest on the ground, a hard one crumples you. Fly outward through seven worlds.
